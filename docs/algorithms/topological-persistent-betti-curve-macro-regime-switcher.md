## In one paragraph

Build multi-scale Betti curves from sliding correlation point clouds and
switch macro allocations before structural crises. It is RK255's crash
warning with Betti curves in place of landscape norms; the landscape
version has the in-sample evidence, and the CSV's reference, arXiv
2004.01234, could not be identified.

## Evidence

* **Gidea & Katz (2018), *Physica A* 491:** persistence-landscape norms
  of four US indices rose before the 2000 and 2008 crashes, in sample
  (see RK255).
* **No study found** that tests this combination as a trading strategy.

## Against, and failure modes

* Betti curves of small clouds are step functions dominated by noise.

## Fit with volatility-ai

A regime input would have to beat the NATR regime; only RK255's
landscape norm has evidence enough to be worth that test.
